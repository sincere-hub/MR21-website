Who We Are

A trusted name in dignified funeral cover.

MR21 Funerals & Insurance is a division of MR21 Burial Society and Funeral Services (Pty) Ltd. Registration number 2018/063193/07.

We serve families with funeral cover and funeral services designed around dignity, affordability and practical support. Our role is to help clients feel at ease with the planning and execution of the actual funeral event, regardless of financial status.
